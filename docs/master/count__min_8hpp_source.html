296 /*
297 * The serialized sketch binary form has the following structure
298 * Byte 0:
299 * 1 - if and only if the sketch is empty
300 * 0 - otherwise
301 *
302 * Byte 1 (serial version), byte 2 (family id), byte 3 (flags):
303 * 00000001 - default for now.
304 *
305 * Bytes 4 - 7:
306 * uint8_t zero corresponding to ``empty''
307 *
308 * Byte 8:
309 * uint_8 for number of hash functions
310 *
311 * Bytes 9, 13
312 * 4 bytes : uint32 for number of buckets.
313 *
314 * Bytes 14, 15:
315 * seed_hash
316 *
317 * Byte 16:
318 * uint8_t zero corresponding to ``empty''
319 *
320 * All remaining bytes from 17-24 follow the pattern of
321 * Bytes 17-24:
322 * Sketch array entry
323 *
324
325 0 || 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
326 ||is_empty|ser__ver|familyId| flags |xxxxxxxx|xxxxxxxx|xxxxxxxx|xxxxxxxx|
327
328 1 || 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
329 ||---------- _num_buckets -----------|num_hash|__seed__ __hash__|xxxxxxxx|
330
331 2 || 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
332 ||---------------------------- total weight ----------------------------|
333
334 3 || 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
335 ||---------------------------- sketch entries ---------------------------|
336 ...
337
338 */
